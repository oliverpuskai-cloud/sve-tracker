Junimo Planner is a free planner and progress tracker for Stardew Valley and Stardew Valley Expanded (SVE). See which fish you can catch today by season, weather and time, tick off Community Center bundles (standard, SVE Normal and SVE Hard), track your museum donations and shipping collection, look up villager birthdays and loved gifts, plan which crops to plant and whether to sell them raw or make wine and jelly, find every cooking and crafting recipe with its ingredients, and see every fishing spot on the map. Progress is saved per farm, in your browser or synced with a Google sign-in.

Fan-made, free and not affiliated with ConcernedApe or FlashShifter. Fish and item sprites and the valley map © ConcernedApe; SVE sprites © FlashShifter, via stardewvalleywiki.com and stardewvalleyexpanded.wiki.gg. Signing in is optional; it stores your Google email and your progress with Supabase only so it can sync (privacy). Fish data: SVE wiki + Stardew Valley wiki (game 1.6). Bundles: standard set from the Stardew Valley wiki, SVE Normal and Hard sets from the SVE wiki.
