Privacy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Junimo Planner is a free, fan-made planner and progress tracker for Stardew Valley and Stardew Valley Expanded. This page explains what it stores and why. The short version: nothing leaves your browser unless you choose to sign in, and even then only your email address and your tracker progress are stored.

Without signing in

Your farms, ticked fish, bundles and settings are saved in your own browser (its local storage). They are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes them, which is why the Backup tab lets you download a copy.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets your progress sync between your devices. When you sign in:

  • Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the site's login service. The site only uses your email address, to show who is signed in.
  • Your tracker progress (farm names, ticked fish and bundle items, chosen season/day/weather) is stored so it can sync.

This data is stored with Supabase, the database and login service the site uses, on servers in the EU (Frankfurt). Database rules make sure each person can only read and change their own progress.

What the site does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone.
  • The site does not access your Google Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open the Backup tab and click Delete my cloud save. That removes your progress from the database and signs you out. To have your login account removed as well, or for any other question, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Fonts and images

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's font servers. Game sprites and the map are hosted on the site itself.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
